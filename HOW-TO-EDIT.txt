HOW TO PERSONALISE THE 20TH ANNIVERSARY WEBSITE
================================================

1) PREVIEW: double-click index.html to open it in your browser.

2) SCHOOL NAME, EVENT DATE, SONG
   Open index.html in a text editor, scroll near the bottom and find:
       const SCHOOL = { name: "...", eventDate: "...", song: "..." }
   Change the name and event date. The name updates everywhere on the page.

3) PHOTOS: put your photos in the "images" folder with EXACTLY these names:
     logo.png          - school logo (top-left)
     hero.jpg          - big background photo (school building)
     principal.jpg     - principal's photo
     achievement-1.jpg ... achievement-6.jpg  - one per achievement card
     gallery-1.jpg ... gallery-9.jpg          - memories gallery
     person-1.jpg ... person-4.jpg            - faces for the messages section
   Each placeholder on the page shows the file name it needs.
   (If your photo is .png or .jpeg, rename it to .jpg or change the name in index.html.)

4) SONG: put your song in the "music" folder named  song.mp3
   It starts when visitors click "Enter the Celebration".
   Until you add it, a built-in cheerful tune plays instead.

5) TEXT: achievements, timeline years, stats numbers, captions and messages
   are all plain text in index.html. Search for the words you want to change.
   Stats numbers are in data-count="..." (e.g. data-count="3500").

Our Story

A message from our Proprietress

Twenty years ago, a dream was planted…

In 2006, JUBLAD opened its doors with a handful of pupils, a small team of passionate teachers, and one big dream: to give every child a place where they are known, loved and inspired to become their very best.

Today, as we celebrate two decades, we look back with gratitude at the thousands of children who have walked through our gates, the teachers who poured their hearts into every lesson, and above all, you, our parents, whose trust and support made all of this possible.

Thank you for being part of our family. The best is yet to come!

Alhaja Fatimat Oyelade
Proprietress

Our 20-Year Journey

The milestones that shaped who we are

2006

The Beginning

Our school opened its doors to its very first pupils, with a vision to nurture bright, confident young minds.

2009

Our First Graduates

We proudly celebrated our very first graduating class, the first of many success stories.

2012

New Building & Library

We expanded our campus with new classrooms and a fully stocked library to spark a love of reading.

2015

Science & ICT Labs

Modern science and computer laboratories opened, bringing hands-on learning to every pupil.

2018

National Recognition

Our pupils earned top honours at national competitions, putting our school on the map.

2021

Stronger Together

Through challenging times, our community adapted, innovated and kept learning alive.

2026

20 Years of Excellence 🎉

Today we celebrate two decades of growth, success and love, and look forward to an even brighter future.
